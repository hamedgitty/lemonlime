# Code of conduct

We want LemonLime to be a friendly place to work on software, whoever you are.

**Expected:** be kind and patient, assume good intent, give and take feedback about the
work rather than the person, and help newcomers find their feet.

**Not okay:** harassment, insults or personal attacks, discriminatory jokes or language,
sexual content or attention, publishing someone's private information, and sustained
disruption of discussions.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space, and when
you represent the project elsewhere.

**Reporting:** tell the maintainers privately through the repository's Security tab
(private reporting) or by email to the maintainer listed on the GitHub profile. Reports
are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, lock threads, or ban people
who break these rules.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
